**Supplementary Figure S3 | DiBiMa-SR from lead II: one-step versus DDIM-6 sampling.**

DiBiMa-SR (one network, DDPM with T = 20 steps) reconstructs the 11 missing leads from lead II either in one step (one network evaluation) or with a 6-step DDIM sampler (six evaluations). Cohorts: PTB-XL test (n = 2,198 records), Georgia (n = 10,275) and China (n = 43,559). **a**, Median per-window PCC of each predicted lead (the vertical grey line separates limb from chest leads). **b**, Mean per-window MAE (z units) of each predicted lead. Shared y axis within each row. **c**, **d**, Distribution of per-record paired differences, one-step minus DDIM-6, of PCC (**c**) and MAE (**d**) over all 11 predicted leads (per-record value = mean over the predicted leads, averaged over the record's two 5-s windows). Violins are drawn over a common display range spanning the 1st–99th percentiles of every cohort (records outside it: 684 for PCC and 528 for MAE, all cohorts, listed per cohort in the source data); dots and bars: mean difference with 95% CI (record bootstrap, B = 1,000; the CIs are narrower than the markers). Values (mean, 95% CI): PTB-XL: ΔPCC +0.0390 (+0.0378 to +0.0402); one-step better in 91.2% of records; Wilcoxon p < 10⁻³⁰⁰ (floating-point underflow); ΔMAE −0.0555 (−0.0562 to −0.0548); one-step better in 99.8% of records; Wilcoxon p < 10⁻³⁰⁰ (floating-point underflow). Georgia: ΔPCC +0.0342 (+0.0336 to +0.0348); one-step better in 86.9% of records; Wilcoxon p < 10⁻³⁰⁰ (floating-point underflow); ΔMAE −0.0532 (−0.0536 to −0.0528); one-step better in 99.3% of records; Wilcoxon p < 10⁻³⁰⁰ (floating-point underflow). China: ΔPCC +0.0329 (+0.0326 to +0.0332); one-step better in 87.6% of records; Wilcoxon p < 10⁻³⁰⁰ (floating-point underflow); ΔMAE −0.0490 (−0.0492 to −0.0489); one-step better in 99.7% of records; Wilcoxon p < 10⁻³⁰⁰ (floating-point underflow). Chest leads only (V1–V6), ΔPCC: PTB-XL +0.0352 (+0.0340 to +0.0364). Georgia +0.0321 (+0.0315 to +0.0328). China +0.0367 (+0.0364 to +0.0370).

Source data: source_data/FigS3_ab_per_lead.csv, FigS3_cd_paired_display.csv, FigS3_paired_onestep_vs_ddim6_all_groups.csv (all, limb and chest groups).
