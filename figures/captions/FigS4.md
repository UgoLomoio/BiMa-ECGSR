**Supplementary Figure S4 | Per-label AUROC on real and reconstructed ECGs for every input configuration and cohort.**

The 12-lead classifier trained only on real PTB-XL ECGs (folds 1–8) is applied unchanged to real ECGs and to reconstructed ECGs. Record-level AUROC (mean score of the two 5-s windows of a record) with 95% CI (record bootstrap, B = 1,000). Columns: input configuration. **a**, PTB-XL test, diagnostic superclasses (n = 2,158 records with a superclass label). **b**, PTB-XL test, 12-label panel (n = 2,198 records). **c**, Georgia, panel (n = 10,275). **d**, China (Chapman-Shaoxing/Ningbo), panel (n = 43,559). Black tick and line: real 12-lead ECG AUROC and 95% CI (identical across columns); orange circles: BiMa-SR; blue triangles: DiBiMa-SR one-step (II only); grey squares: linear filter (I, II); grey diamonds: Einthoven + linear filter (configurations containing I and II, whose limb leads are exact). Records with an unknown label are excluded from that label; per-label positive and negative counts are in the source data. Shared x axis within each row. Not scored: PTB-XL AFLT (7 positive records); PTB-XL WPW (8 positive records); Georgia WPW (2 positive records); China AFLT (label not scored in this cohort).

Source data: source_data/FigS4_per_label_auroc.csv.
