**Supplementary Figure FigS_failures | Low-percentile reconstructions (10th percentile).**
Every example is selected by a fixed rule, not by eye: within a group, windows are ranked by a per-window score (BiMa-SR PCC averaged over the scored leads of that window) and the window at the 10th percentile (nearest rank) is shown. This figure complements Figure 3 (same groups, same rule, 90th percentile there). Low-percentile windows include real ECGs with noisy or atypical leads, where neither method follows the recording (PCC below 0 for both BiMa-SR and the linear filter: Georgia V3, -0.362 and -0.159). Traces are 5-s windows at 500 Hz in z units (each lead scaled separately; horizontal bar, 1 s). Black, real ECG; orange, BiMa-SR; dashed grey, linear filter; pink, measured input lead (not predicted). Numbers on the traces are the per-lead PCC of the shown window (orange, BiMa-SR; grey, linear filter), read from the per-window metrics files.
**a**, PTB-XL test, NORM records: score over the 11 predicted leads of the lead II model (1,926 windows / 963 records); selected record 14187, window 1 of 2, score 0.768. The same window is reconstructed by BiMa-SR from each of the six input configurations (rows); chest leads V1, V3 and V5 are shown.
**b**, Input I+II+V1, PTB-XL test, one window per diagnostic label (records labelled MI, CLBBB or LVH; a record may carry more than one label); score over the predicted chest leads V2–V6. MI: record 11286, window 1 of 2, score 0.698 (group of 1,100 windows / 550 records); CLBBB: record 15958, window 2 of 2, score 0.631 (group of 108 windows / 54 records); LVH: record 8259, window 2 of 2, score 0.745 (group of 428 windows / 214 records). The comparator for I+II+V1 is Einthoven + linear filter, whose chest leads are the linear-filter chest leads shown here.
**c**, Input I+II+V1, external cohorts (all windows), same score. Georgia: record E08149, window 2 of 2, score 0.620 (group of 20,547 windows / 10,275 records); China: record JS08712, window 1 of 2, score 0.751 (group of 87,105 windows / 43,559 records).
Source data: source_data/FigS_failures_selected_windows.csv (selection value, record identifiers and per-lead PCCs).
