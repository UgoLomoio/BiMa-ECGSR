**Figure 4 | Diagnostic readability of reconstructed ECGs by a classifier trained on real ECGs only.**

One 12-lead 1D-ResNet classifier (3.36 M parameters), trained only on real PTB-XL ECGs (folds 1–8), is applied unchanged to real 12-lead ECGs and to 12-lead ECGs reconstructed from each input configuration (measured input leads re-inserted). Scores are averaged over the two 5-s windows of a record; all AUROCs are record level. Cohorts: PTB-XL test fold 10 (n = 2,198 records; 2,158 with a diagnostic superclass label), Georgia (external, n = 10,275) and China (Chapman-Shaoxing/Ningbo, external, n = 43,559); records with an unknown label are excluded from that label. 95% CIs: record bootstrap, B = 1,000, identical resamples for every input type. Baseline: linear filter (per-lead 101-tap FIR, least squares) for I and II; Einthoven + linear filter (exact Einthoven/Goldberger limb leads, linear filter for chest leads) for configurations containing I and II.

**a**, Macro AUROC (95% CI) versus input configuration for BiMa-SR (orange circles), the baseline (grey squares: linear filter; grey diamonds: Einthoven + linear filter) and DiBiMa-SR one-step (blue triangle, II only), on the PTB-XL diagnostic superclasses (NORM, MI, STTC, CD, HYP) and on the 12-label rhythm/conduction/hypertrophy panel (AFIB, AFLT, 1AVB, PAC, PVC, SBRAD, STACH, WPW, LVH, RVH, CRBBB, CLBBB) of PTB-XL, Georgia and China. Dashed line and band: AUROC on the real 12-lead ECGs and its 95% CI. Macro AUROC averages the scored labels; labels with fewer than 10 positive records are skipped (PTB-XL AFLT (7 positive records); PTB-XL WPW (8 positive records); Georgia WPW (2 positive records); China AFLT (not scored)). Example: PTB-XL superclasses, real 0.912 (0.903–0.920); from lead II alone BiMa-SR 0.843 (0.832–0.854), linear filter 0.787 (0.774–0.800); from I+II+V1+V4 BiMa-SR 0.908 (0.899–0.916).

**b**, Difference in macro AUROC between reconstructed and real ECGs (mean over the paired bootstrap resamples, 95% percentile CI; B = 1,000). Filled markers: CI excludes 0; open markers: CI includes 0 (difference from the real-ECG AUROC not significant at the 5% level). Shared y axis. BiMa-SR differences whose CI includes 0: I+II on PTB-XL, 12-label panel; I+II+V1 on Georgia, panel; I+II+V2 on Georgia, panel; I+II+V1+V4 on Georgia, panel; I+II+V1 on China, panel; I+II+V1+V4 on China, panel. Example, China panel from lead II: BiMa-SR −0.038 (−0.046 to −0.030), linear filter −0.128 (−0.137 to −0.117).

**c**, Per-superclass AUROC of the classifier on PTB-XL ECGs reconstructed by BiMa-SR (one row per input configuration) and on the real 12-lead ECGs (bottom row), n = 2,158 records; positives per class are listed in the source data. Values printed in each cell; colour scale cividis.

**d**, Per-label AUROC difference (reconstructed − real; point estimates, no CI) on the external cohorts for the single-lead configuration II and the 5-electrode configuration I+II+V1. Orange circles: BiMa-SR; grey square: linear filter (II); grey diamond: Einthoven + linear filter (I+II+V1). "not scored": Georgia WPW (fewer than 10 positive records) and China AFLT (label not scored in China). Per-label AUROCs with 95% CIs for every configuration and cohort are in Supplementary Fig. S4.

Source data: source_data/Figure4_a_macro_auroc.csv, Figure4_b_delta_auroc.csv, Figure4_c_superclass_heatmap.csv, Figure4_d_label_deltas.csv.
