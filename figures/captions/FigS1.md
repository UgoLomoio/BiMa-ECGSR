**Supplementary Figure S1 | Validation curves of the seven reconstruction runs.**

Validation pooled median PCC (median over window × lead pairs, z-scored leads) after each training epoch, computed on the fixed set of 1,024 PTB-XL validation windows (fold 9), for the predicted chest leads (solid line, circles) and the predicted limb leads (dashed line, open squares). Epochs run per model: 20. The dotted vertical line marks the selected checkpoint: the epoch with the highest validation pooled median PCC over all predicted leads (argmax of the curve, confirmed against the training log), which is the checkpoint used for every test-set result. Epochs are numbered from 1. DiBiMa-SR (II) was validated with DDIM-6 sampling. For configurations containing I and II, the limb leads III, aVR, aVL and aVF are exactly derivable from the inputs; their validation PCC is near 1 for BiMa-SR, so the chest-lead curve carries the information. **a**, BiMa-SR, I; **b**, BiMa-SR, II; **c**, DiBiMa-SR, II; **d**, BiMa-SR, I+II; **e**, BiMa-SR, I+II+V1; **f**, BiMa-SR, I+II+V2; **g**, BiMa-SR, I+II+V1+V4. Selected epochs and validation PCC at the selected epoch: BiMa-SR, I: epoch 11, chest 0.919, limb 0.889; BiMa-SR, II: epoch 10, chest 0.918, limb 0.910; DiBiMa-SR, II: epoch 11, chest 0.872, limb 0.851; BiMa-SR, I+II: epoch 15, chest 0.940, limb 0.996; BiMa-SR, I+II+V1: epoch 13, chest 0.964, limb 0.997; BiMa-SR, I+II+V2: epoch 13, chest 0.970, limb 0.998; BiMa-SR, I+II+V1+V4: epoch 20, chest 0.979, limb 0.998.

Source data: source_data/FigS1_validation_curves.csv.
