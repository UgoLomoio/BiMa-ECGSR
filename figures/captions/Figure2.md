**Figure 2 | Reconstruction accuracy across input configurations and cohorts.**
Configurations are ordered by the number of measured leads. Metrics are computed on 5-s windows (500 Hz, per-lead z-scored) and on predicted leads only; measured input leads are excluded. Because III, aVR, aVL and aVF are exactly derivable from I and II, the fair comparison for configurations containing I+II is on the predicted chest leads, which is the primary metric in **a** and **c**. Comparator: the linear filter (per-lead FIR, 101 taps per input lead, least squares on 4,000 PTB-XL training windows) for inputs I or II alone, and Einthoven + linear filter (exact Einthoven/Goldberger limb leads, linear filter for chest leads) for configurations containing I and II; on chest leads the two comparators are identical. Cohorts: PTB-XL test 2,198 records / 4,396 windows; Georgia 10,275 records / 20,547 windows; China 43,559 records / 87,105 windows.
**a**, Chest-lead pooled median Pearson correlation coefficient (PCC; median over all window × predicted-chest-lead pairs) with 95% record-bootstrap confidence intervals (B = 1,000; both windows of a record resampled together); most intervals are narrower than the markers. On PTB-XL test, BiMa-SR reaches 0.919 (input I) to 0.979 (I+II+V1+V4), versus 0.783 to 0.962 for the comparator.
**b**, Per-lead pooled median PCC of BiMa-SR on PTB-XL test (left; n = 4,396 windows per cell) and BiMa-SR minus comparator (right; diverging scale centred at 0). Pink cells, measured input leads (not evaluated). The vertical line separates limb from chest leads. For configurations containing I+II, the Einthoven + linear filter comparator is exact on limb leads (per-lead PCC > 0.9999 on PTB-XL test; the residual is numerical), so the small negative limb differences are expected; for the same reason the all-predicted-lead PCC for I+II+V1+V4 on PTB-XL test is lower for BiMa-SR (0.995) than for Einthoven + linear filter (0.999), although BiMa-SR is higher on chest leads.
**c**, Chest-lead mean absolute error (MAE, z units; mean over window × lead pairs) with 95% record-bootstrap CIs (B = 1,000).
**d**, Lead II input only. Left, pooled median PCC over all 11 predicted leads with 95% record-bootstrap CIs for BiMa-SR (regression), DiBiMa-SR (diffusion, DDPM T = 20) sampled in one step or with DDIM-6, and the linear filter. Right, paired per-record difference in PCC (per-record PCC = mean over a record's predicted leads and windows), BiMa-SR minus DiBiMa-SR one-step: mean difference [95% record-bootstrap CI]; the label above each point is the percentage of records in which BiMa-SR has the higher per-record PCC. PTB-XL test +0.0172 [+0.0145, +0.0197], 63% of records, Wilcoxon P = 3.2e-48; Georgia +0.0121 [+0.0107, +0.0136], 58% of records, Wilcoxon P = 8.5e-71; China +0.0243 [+0.0236, +0.0249], 66% of records, Wilcoxon P < 10⁻³⁰⁰.
Source data: source_data/Figure2_a_chest_pcc.csv, Figure2_b_per_lead_ptbxl_test.csv, Figure2_c_chest_mae.csv, Figure2_d_leadII_models.csv.
