**Graphical abstract | Reconstructing the 12-lead ECG from reduced lead sets with BiMa-SR.**
**a**, One example. Three measured leads, I, II and V1 (pink), which a 5-electrode bedside telemetry montage provides, are the only input to BiMa-SR, a bidirectional-Mamba encoder–decoder (regression, one forward pass; 2.68 M parameters). BiMa-SR outputs the 9 missing leads. Shown are the predicted chest leads V2–V6 (orange) overlaid on the real ECG (black); the predicted limb leads III, aVR, aVL and aVF are not shown. 2.5 s of the 5-s, 500-Hz window are drawn, and signals are per-lead z-scored. Selection rule (not by eye): the PTB-XL test window at the 90th percentile of BiMa-SR per-window PCC, the PCC being averaged over the 9 predicted leads (rank ⌊0.90 × 4,396⌋ of 4,396 windows). This is PTB-XL ecg_id 21099, first 5-s half, superclass STTC, per-window PCC 0.99 (per-lead PCC 0.97–0.995 for V2–V6).
**b**, Chest-lead accuracy against the number of measured leads. The six input configurations are ordered by the number of measured leads: I, II, I+II, I+II+V1, I+II+V2 and I+II+V1+V4. Each point is the pooled median PCC (median over window × lead pairs) of the predicted precordial leads (V1–V6, measured inputs excluded). BiMa-SR is shown in orange. The best linear baseline is shown in grey: the per-lead linear filter (101-tap FIR per input lead, least squares on 4,000 PTB-XL training windows) or, when I and II are measured, Einthoven + linear filter. The two baselines have identical chest leads, and the Einthoven/Goldberger relations make the latter exact on the limb leads, which is why chest leads are the fair comparison. Cohorts: PTB-XL test, fold 10 (circles; n = 2,198 records, 4,396 windows); Georgia, external (squares; 10,275 records, 20,547 windows); China, Chapman-Shaoxing/Ningbo, external (triangles; 43,559 records, 87,105 windows). BiMa-SR is higher in all 18 configuration–cohort pairs, by 0.012 to 0.136.
**c**, Diagnostic readability on PTB-XL. One 12-lead 1D-ResNet classifier (3.36 M parameters), trained only on real PTB-XL ECGs (folds 1–8), is run unchanged on the reconstructed 12-lead ECGs. The plotted value is the record-level macro AUROC over the five PTB-XL superclasses (NORM, MI, STTC, CD, HYP; 2,158 test records with at least one superclass label). Dashed line: the same classifier on real 12-lead ECGs (0.912). With three or more measured leads, BiMa-SR reconstructions score 0.004–0.011 below real ECGs.
**d**, The same analysis for the external cohorts. The plotted value is macro AUROC over the 12-label rhythm/morphology panel (AFIB, AFLT, 1AVB, PAC, PVC, SBRAD, STACH, WPW, LVH, RVH, CRBBB, CLBBB; labels with < 10 positive records skipped; AFLT never scored in China). Georgia is shown as squares (real ECG 0.871) and China as triangles (real ECG 0.926); each dashed line is the real-ECG reference of its cohort.
Error bars: 95% record-bootstrap CIs (B = 1,000); in **b** they are no wider than the markers (≤ 0.014). Bottom line: the takeaway, computed by the figure script from the plotted values. Single training seed per model. Source data: source_data/Graphical_Abstract_example.csv and _example_lead_pcc.csv (a), _fidelity.csv (b), _auroc.csv (c, d), _takeaway.csv.
