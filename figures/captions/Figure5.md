**Figure 5 | Generalisation, diagnostic subgroups and efficiency.**
Accuracy is the chest-lead pooled median PCC (median over window × predicted-chest-lead pairs, z-scored 5-s windows); configurations are ordered by the number of measured leads. Chest leads are used for every configuration, including I and II, for consistency with Figure 2a; limb leads are excluded because the Einthoven + linear filter comparator is exact on them for configurations containing I+II. Comparator: linear filter for inputs I or II alone; Einthoven + linear filter otherwise (identical chest leads).
**a**, PTB-XL test, per diagnostic superclass (NORM, normal; MI, myocardial infarction; STTC, ST/T change; CD, conduction disturbance; HYP, hypertrophy); a record contributes to every superclass it carries, and the number under each label is the number of records. Left, BiMa-SR; right, BiMa-SR minus comparator (diverging scale centred at 0). BiMa-SR is higher than the comparator in all 30 cells (smallest difference +0.013, largest +0.210).
**b**, External-cohort change: pooled median PCC on Georgia (10,275 records) or China (43,559 records) minus PTB-XL test (2,198 records), for BiMa-SR and the comparator. 95% CIs: percentile intervals of the difference between independent record bootstraps of the two cohorts (B = 1,000 each, same resamples as the single-cohort CIs). Both methods lose accuracy on the external cohorts. The BiMa-SR drop is larger than the comparator's (non-overlapping CIs) for I (Georgia), II (Georgia), I+II (Georgia), I+II+V1 (Georgia), I+II+V2 (Georgia), I (China), II (China), and smaller for I+II+V1 (China). BiMa-SR remains above the comparator in absolute chest-lead PCC in every configuration and external cohort (Figure 2a). Examples: input I, Georgia -0.070 [-0.074, -0.067] for BiMa-SR versus -0.043 [-0.051, -0.035] for the linear filter; I+II+V1+V4, China -0.014 [-0.015, -0.013] versus -0.015 [-0.017, -0.014].
**c**, Efficiency (from results/final/efficiency.csv). Markers: lead-II models; vertical ticks: the other five input configurations (BiMa-SR 2,675,148–2,676,108 parameters, linear filter 1,111–3,232 coefficients = 101 taps × input leads × predicted leads). GPU latency is the time per 10-s record (batch of its two 5-s windows); throughput is windows per second at batch 128; CPU latency is per 10-s record with 2 threads for the neural models and 1 thread for the linear filter (the only CPU setting timed for it). The linear filter was not timed on GPU. Lead II: BiMa-SR 45.8 ms (GPU) and 1,293 ms (CPU); DiBiMa-SR one-step 46.9 ms and 1,482 ms; DDIM-6 (6 network evaluations) 274.5 ms and 9,255 ms; linear filter 1.07 ms (CPU, 1 thread). Hardware and timing protocol as in Methods.
Source data: source_data/Figure5_a_superclass.csv, Figure5_b_external_drop.csv, Figure5_c_efficiency.csv.
