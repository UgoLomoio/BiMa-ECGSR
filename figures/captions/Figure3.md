**Figure 3 | Qualitative reconstructions.**
Every example is selected by a fixed rule, not by eye: within a group, windows are ranked by a per-window score (BiMa-SR PCC averaged over the scored leads of that window) and the window at the 90th percentile (nearest rank) is shown. The 90th percentile shows a good but typical case, not the best one; the same groups at the 10th percentile are shown in Supplementary FigS_failures. Traces are 5-s windows at 500 Hz in z units (each lead scaled separately; horizontal bar, 1 s). Black, real ECG; orange, BiMa-SR; dashed grey, linear filter; pink, measured input lead (not predicted). Numbers on the traces are the per-lead PCC of the shown window (orange, BiMa-SR; grey, linear filter), read from the per-window metrics files.
**a**, PTB-XL test, NORM records: score over the 11 predicted leads of the lead II model (1,926 windows / 963 records); selected record 417, window 1 of 2, score 0.936. The same window is reconstructed by BiMa-SR from each of the six input configurations (rows); chest leads V1, V3 and V5 are shown.
**b**, Input I+II+V1, PTB-XL test, one window per diagnostic label (records labelled MI, CLBBB or LVH; a record may carry more than one label); score over the predicted chest leads V2–V6. MI: record 17007, window 1 of 2, score 0.968 (group of 1,100 windows / 550 records); CLBBB: record 7919, window 1 of 2, score 0.950 (group of 108 windows / 54 records); LVH: record 1386, window 1 of 2, score 0.972 (group of 428 windows / 214 records). The comparator for I+II+V1 is Einthoven + linear filter, whose chest leads are the linear-filter chest leads shown here.
**c**, Input I+II+V1, external cohorts (all windows), same score. Georgia: record E09552, window 1 of 2, score 0.964 (group of 20,547 windows / 10,275 records); China: record JS34127, window 1 of 2, score 0.971 (group of 87,105 windows / 43,559 records).
Source data: source_data/Figure3_selected_windows.csv (selection value, record identifiers and per-lead PCCs).
