**Supplementary Figure S2 | Per-lead reconstruction accuracy on the external cohorts.**

Median per-window PCC (z-scored 5-s windows) of each reconstructed lead, per input configuration (rows) and lead (columns), on Georgia (n = 10,275 records, 20,547 windows) and China (Chapman-Shaoxing/Ningbo; n = 43,559 records, 87,105 windows). **a**, Georgia, BiMa-SR. **b**, Georgia, baseline. **c**, China, BiMa-SR. **d**, China, baseline. Baseline: linear filter (LF; per-lead 101-tap FIR fitted by least squares on 4,000 PTB-XL training windows) for I and II; Einthoven + linear filter (exact Einthoven/Goldberger limb leads, LF for the chest leads) for configurations containing I and II. Hatched cells marked "input": measured leads, not evaluated. The white vertical line separates limb (left) and chest (right) leads. For configurations containing I and II the limb leads III, aVR, aVL and aVF are exactly derivable, so the Einthoven + linear filter limb cells are ≈1 by construction and the chest-lead cells are the fair comparison. Common colour scale across panels (cividis, 0.5–1; cells below 0.5, 5 in total, all baseline cells, take the floor colour and keep their printed value). Values in each cell.

Source data: source_data/FigS2_per_lead_pcc.csv (also contains the per-lead mean MAE).
